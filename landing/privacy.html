Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

At PopVerse, your privacy is a foundational priority. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your personal data when you use the PopVerse mobile application, website, and related services ("Service"), as well as your statutory privacy rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and other applicable legislation.

Our Core Privacy Commitments:
• We DO NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.
• We DO NOT track your activity across third-party websites or services for advertising.
• Your personal library, watch history, and lists remain strictly your private property.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect data strictly necessary to provide and enhance your entertainment tracking experience:

  • Account & Profile Information: When signing in (via Google Sign-In or Firebase Authentication), we store your unique User ID (UID), email address, display name, and avatar URL.
  • Watchlist & Progress Data: Titles marked as watched, ratings, episode progress, custom watchlists, and watch streak records stored in your personal profile.
  • User-Generated Submissions: Bug reports, feature suggestions, comments, votes, and user-uploaded screenshots (processed securely via Cloudinary).
  • Diagnostic & Device Data: App version, device platform (iOS / Android), and anonymized error logs used exclusively to investigate crashes and performance bugs.
  • Data We Never Collect: We never collect payment card information, government identifiers, biometric data, or precise background GPS tracking.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly on the basis of service performance and legitimate interest:

  • Cross-Device Synchronization: Ensuring your watchlists and episode progress remain synced in real time across all your devices.
  • Airdate & Release Alerts: Sending you timely notifications when new episodes air or movies premiere.
  • Personalized Statistics: Calculating total watch hours, active watch streaks, and viewing habit breakdowns.
  • Platform Security & Bug Resolution: Analyzing crashes and resolving technical issues reported by users.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

PopVerse partners with vetted third-party infrastructure providers to operate the Service:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore): Manages secure user authentication, real-time database storage, and encryption.
  • The Movie Database (TMDb): Powers media search, plot overviews, episode guides, and poster artwork via public APIs.
  • Cloudinary: Secure cloud asset storage for user-submitted feedback attachments.
  • YouTube API Services: Streams official movie and show trailers in-app in compliance with YouTube terms.
  • Web3Forms: Relays feedback notifications to administrators without tracking end users.

4. Data Storage & Security Safeguards

We maintain comprehensive industry-grade technical and organizational safeguards:

  • Transport Encryption: All network communication is strictly enforced over TLS 1.3/SSL (HTTPS).
  • At-Rest Encryption: Database records and backups are encrypted using AES-256 via Google Cloud infrastructure.
  • Strict Security Rules: Granular Firestore security rules restrict read and write access, ensuring only authenticated account owners can access their private library.
  • No Client Secret Keys: Cloud storage operations use scoped, unsigned presets so no administrative credentials exist within the mobile app.

5. Data Retention & Your Deletion Rights

We retain your information only for as long as your account remains active. You hold full ownership of your data:

  • Data Export: You can export your complete watch history and lists to CSV format at any time using the in-app export utility.
  • Account Deletion: You can permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data directly in the Settings page or by contacting mjwaghmare.mtw@gmail.com. Account deletion immediately and irreversibly purges your Firestore records.

6. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Under applicable international privacy laws, you possess the following enforceable rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a complete copy of all personal information we store about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Update or correct inaccurate account details at any time.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to Be Forgotten): Request the permanent removal of your account and personal data.
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We never discriminate or degrade service quality for exercising your privacy rights.

To exercise any of these statutory rights, please email us at mjwaghmare.mtw@gmail.com. Requests are handled promptly within 30 days free of charge.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

PopVerse is not intended for use by children under 13 years of age (or 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you become aware that a child has created an account without parental consent, please contact us immediately for prompt deletion.

8. Policy Modifications

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to accommodate evolving privacy regulations or new app capabilities. Revisions will be published with an updated "Effective Date" at the top of this document.
